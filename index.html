    Privacy
    
      
    Recording and transcribing happen on this phone. Audio is never uploaded: the only thing that leaves the phone is the transcript, when you tap Send to WorkFlow, and it goes to your own WorkFlow in your school Google account. To check, turn on airplane mode after the model is downloaded; transcription still works.
    
      
    Speech model: NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B (CC BY 4.0), run with parakeet.js and ONNX Runtime Web (MIT).
